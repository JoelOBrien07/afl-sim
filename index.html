    Pick a club and an outcome, then start.

    How it works. Each game starts from an expected margin, taken from the chosen prediction source (Squiggle models by default) or, where there is none, from the rating gap between the two clubs plus a home ground edge. Random noise is then added to produce a final margin and scores. Wins are worth 4 premiership points, draws 2, and the ladder is ordered by points then percentage. The top eight play the finals series, simulated at neutral venues.
